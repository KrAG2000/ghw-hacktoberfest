## What Cursor helped create

Cursor built the Campus Week Board, a one-page app for adding campus events with a name, time, location, and audience, then filtering that list and keeping it sorted soonest first. It also wired dark mode, localStorage persistence, an empty state, and the date rules in events.mjs that the tests cover.
